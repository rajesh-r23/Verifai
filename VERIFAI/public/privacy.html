Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information VERIFAI handles and how we protect it.

On this page

1. Information we handle 2. How we use it 3. Storage & retention 4. Sharing 5. Cookies 6. Security 7. Your rights 8. Children 9. Changes 10. Contact

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Information we handle

When you use VERIFAI, we may handle the following:

  • Content you submit for analysis such as images, SMS text or email text.
  • Account details such as your name and email address, if you create an account.
  • Contact messages you send us through the contact form.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device information.

2. How we use it

  • To analyse the content you submit and show you the result.
  • To run, maintain and improve our tools.
  • To reply to your questions and feedback.
  • To keep the platform safe and prevent misuse.

3. Storage & retention

We don't store your personal data from the content you submit for analysis beyond what is needed to produce your result. Contact messages and account details are kept only as long as needed for the purpose they were provided.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with trusted service providers that help us operate the platform, or when required by law.

5. Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to keep the site working properly and to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. To make a request, please contact us.

8. Children

VERIFAI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our Contact page or at support@verifai.com.
